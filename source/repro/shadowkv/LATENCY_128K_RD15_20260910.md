# Qwen3 128K latency gate, fixed center price 1.5

Measured on 2026-09-10 on machine 4 (`sashimi`), one RTX 4090 (24 GiB),
batch size one, Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, one real 128K RULER CWE prompt, and
nominal retrieval budget 4096.  Each successful row loaded the model once,
ran one complete warm-up pass, then measured one prefill and 32 decode steps.
Methods ran sequentially on an otherwise idle GPU.  Ours, Quest, and ParisKV
all retain 4096 routed tokens plus 32 prefix/sink and 32 recent tokens (4160
effective tokens).  Released ShadowKV retains 4096 routed tokens plus 384
scaled outlier tokens and 32 local tokens (4512 effective tokens).

| Method | Prefill (s) | Decode median (ms/token) | Decode mean (ms/token) | Mean throughput (token/s) | Peak GPU (GiB) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Ours, query-mean, fixed price 1.5 | 48.99 | 35.66 | 55.24 | 18.10 | 18.74 |
| Quest-streaming, page 16, dense layers 0 | 45.81 | 50.39 | 50.22 | 19.91 | 15.86 |
| Released ShadowKV-CPU, rank 160 | 52.10 | 30.96 | 31.19 | 32.07 | 14.30 |
| ParisKV authors' runtime | 47.44 | -- | 89.05 | 11.23 | 17.64 |
| Full attention | OOM | OOM | OOM | OOM | >24 GiB required |

Ours selected 1.924 centers per block on this prompt.  Its ordinary decode
steps are fast, but sealing/updating a streaming block produced a maximum
194.79 ms step, so the mean is the honest throughput number.  Against
ParisKV, ours is 2.50x faster by median ordinary-step latency and 1.61x faster
by mean throughput.  Against Quest d0, ours is 1.41x faster on an ordinary
step but 10.0% slower in mean throughput because of update spikes.  Released
ShadowKV is 1.77x faster than ours by mean decode latency, while attending 352
more effective tokens.

Quest's paper-style `dense_layers=2` row is not reported: the current
streaming implementation rejects dense layers with CPU-offloaded KV before
measurement.  Full attention completed prefill but failed when moving the
full K/V cache to GPU: it requested another 9.14 GiB with only 5.52 GiB free.
